# How to Read BMKG Weather Forecasts in the Info BMKG App

"Cuaca" (weather) is the most searched word in Indonesia in the past year of Google Trends data, with "cuaca besok" (tomorrow's weather) also near the top. People check the forecast before commuting, drying laundry, travelling, or holding an event. The official source in Indonesia is the Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysics Agency (BMKG). This article explains how to read a **BMKG weather forecast**, especially through the Info BMKG app, so you do more than glance at a cloud icon.

A note: the app's features and appearance can change. The information below is based on what was available when this article was written. Check the app store or BMKG's official site for the latest.

## What Info BMKG Offers

Info BMKG is BMKG's official app, available on Google Play and the App Store, and also as a web version. According to the information we found, its features include:

- **Weather forecasts** for several days ahead, broken down by district.
- **Early weather warnings** for areas likely to see significant weather.
- **Earthquake information** and related notifications.
- **Aviation and maritime weather** for specialized needs.
- **UV index** and other supporting information.

Download only from official app stores and check that the developer is BMKG. Many other weather apps can be used too, but BMKG is the official authority in Indonesia.

## Choosing the Right Location

Weather can differ greatly between areas, even within one city. Choose the most detailed location available, such as your district or ward, not just the city name. If you are travelling, check your destination and the places along the way, not only where you live.

## Reading the Forecast Elements

1. **Weather condition.** The icon and description, such as partly cloudy, cloudy, or light rain. This is a summary, not a guarantee of rain or sun all the time.
2. **Temperature.** The estimated air temperature. It can feel different depending on humidity and wind.
3. **Air humidity.** High humidity makes the air feel muggier and supports the formation of rain clouds.
4. **Wind speed and direction.** Important for motorcyclists, sailing, and outdoor activities.
5. **Time by hour or part of the day.** Weather can change within a day, such as sunny in the morning and rainy in the afternoon. Look at the times, not just the daily status.

## A Forecast Is an Estimate

This is what often disappoints people. A weather forecast describes likelihood based on data and models, not certainty. A few things to remember:

- **The further out the day, the less certain.** A forecast for tomorrow is generally more reliable than one several days away.
- **Local weather is hard to predict.** Convective rain can be very local and brief. One district gets heavy rain while the neighbouring one stays dry.
- **Regular updates.** Forecasts get updated, so recheck before important activities.
- **Combine with your own observation.** Thickening dark clouds or a sudden strong wind are signs you should notice yourself.

## Early Warnings: Do Not Ignore Them

Besides routine forecasts, BMKG issues early warnings for potentially impactful weather, such as heavy rain, strong winds, and high waves. Turn on notifications in the app so warnings arrive on time. When a warning covers your area:

1. Postpone non-urgent trips.
2. Avoid sheltering under large trees or billboards, and stay away from flood- or landslide-prone areas.
3. Secure items outside the house and make sure your phone and power bank are charged.
4. Follow instructions from local authorities.

For fuller preparation for the rainy season, also read our guide on flood preparedness.

## Using Forecasts for Daily Plans

- **Commuting to work or school:** check the forecast for departure and return times. Bring a raincoat or umbrella if rain is possible.
- **Drying laundry or crops:** look at the time segments, not just the daily status.
- **Long trips:** check the destination, route, and the forecast for the next several days, then check again on departure day.
- **Outdoor events:** prepare a backup plan under a roof, since a forecast is not a guarantee.
- **Activities at sea:** check maritime weather and wave height information, and follow harbour authority advisories.

## Beware of Fake Weather Information

Ahead of extreme seasons, chain messages about a "huge storm" or "extreme weather on such-and-such date" often circulate without a clear source. Check BMKG's official channels directly before sharing. If the source cannot be proven, do not spread it. You can use the steps in our article on checking hoaxes.

## Common Mistakes

- Looking only at the icon without reading the times and other elements.
- Treating a forecast several days ahead as certain.
- Checking the wrong location, such as only the city when you are on its outskirts.
- Turning off early warning notifications.
- Trusting chain messages without an official source.

## Conclusion

BMKG weather forecasts in the Info BMKG app help you plan your day more calmly, as long as you read them correctly. Choose a detailed location, watch the times and elements such as temperature, humidity, and wind, remember that a forecast is an estimate, and turn on early warning notifications. With those habits, weather becomes useful information, not just a surprise.
